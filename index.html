Run python palm_bridge.py locally first and allow webcam access. Whichever steering source is selected above keeps flying the drone (WASD/tilt, Bluetooth IMU, or USB) — palm tracking only overrides look direction and fire: your left palm aims (tilt/height), your right palm pinches thumb+index to fire — each hand does only its own job. Mouse-look/click still work as a fallback whenever the left hand isn't tracked. Once you launch, the camera feed itself shows up in a small preview panel in the corner (no separate window opens on the machine running the bridge) — run with --headless instead if you'd rather skip that and save bandwidth.

tilt/WASD steers left-right & climb-dive  |  G deploy arms  |  H retract arms  |  F draw/holster pistols  |  mouse looks around (360°) to aim  |  X or click to fire  |  V cycles chase/orbit/first-person camera  |  scroll zoom  |  ESC menu
